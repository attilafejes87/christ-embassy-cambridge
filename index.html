FIRST TIME WITH US?

A few things to know.

What can I expect?

Our services bring together contemporary worship, prayer and Bible teaching. You’re welcome to join in and experience the service at your own pace.

What should I wear?

There’s no specific dress code. Our church family wears a variety of styles, so choose something comfortable and appropriate for you.

Do I have to introduce myself?

No. You won’t be expected to stand up or speak. If you’d like to meet someone or ask a question, simply get in touch before you visit.

Can I join online?

Yes. Visit our online service page for the broadcast and participation details.
